## Perfect 24 hours — city itinerary template

This template fits most walkable cities. Adjust times for local opening hours and travel time.

Copyable one-day checklist

- [ ] Morning coffee + quick landmark walk
- [ ] Main attraction/museum (booked ticket)
- [ ] Scenic stroll or market
- [ ] Lunch at a local spot
- [ ] Short afternoon activity (boat, garden, viewpoint)
- [ ] Explore a secondary neighborhood
- [ ] Early dinner with a local specialty
- [ ] Sunset viewpoint or evening walk
- [ ] Optional evening live music or relaxed bar

Printable time blocks (fill in times)

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 07:00 | Coffee / warm-up walk |
| 08:30 | Main attraction / museum |
| 11:00 | Market / stroll |
| 12:30 | Lunch |
| 14:00 | Short activity (boat/viewpoint) |
| 15:45 | Rest / snack |
| 16:30 | Secondary neighborhood |
| 18:30 | Dinner |
| 20:30 | Sunset / evening walk |

Actionable itinerary rules

1. Prioritize one paid attraction and one free experience (market, park, viewpoint).
2. Buy timed tickets in advance for the main attraction to avoid queues.
3. Use local transit apps to estimate travel time and set 15–30 minute buffers.
4. Reserve dinner or high-demand meals if they’re on your must-see list.
5. Leave one flexible 30–60 minute slot for serendipity.

Packing & logistics tips

- Travel with a small daypack and keep essentials (water, tickets, phone charger) accessible.
- Charge devices overnight and carry a small power bank for long days.
- Download offline maps and a short list of must-see addresses to save time.

Customization notes

- For museums: pick 1–2 galleries and set a 90–120 minute limit.
- Family trips: trade a museum for a playground or interactive museum and add extra snack breaks.
- Food-first trips: add 30–60 minutes per meal and include a food market visit.

Final reminder

This is a flexible blueprint—choose depth (stay longer at one place) or breadth (see more) depending on what you’ll remember most.
